## Long description
Before you ape in, check the contract. Send @Rug1CheckSolBot any Solana token mint address (or a pump.fun / solscan link) and get a verdict in seconds, read straight from the blockchain:

• Mint authority — can someone print more tokens?
• Freeze authority — can your tokens be frozen (honeypot)?
• Permanent delegate — can someone move tokens out of your wallet?
• Transfer hooks & fees — can selling be blocked or taxed?
• Pause switch & changeable metadata
• Holder concentration — how much do the top wallets hold?

Works in groups: add the bot and type /check <mint>.
5 free checks per day; more via Telegram Stars. No wallet connection, no sign-up.
Not financial advice: contract checks can't see liquidity pulls or team behaviour.
